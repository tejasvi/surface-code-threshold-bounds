# QECD1: Lean proofs for the surface code under measurement errors

This Lean 4 project contains the formal proofs of the threshold theorem of the paper for the planar surface code under
measurement errors. In this phenomenological noise model every data qubit flips with probability `p` between rounds
and every syndrome bit of the noisy rounds flips with probability `p`, independently; the final round is perfect. The
project bounds the failure probability of maximum-likelihood decoding by a quantity that tends to 0, exponentially in
the distance, at every `p ≤ 0.02`; this holds under conditions (V6), (Hcov) and (Bin) of the paper, with confidence
1 - 1e-9.

## Build

Requirements: elan (the Lean toolchain manager). From the project root:

    bash scripts/build.sh

This downloads the prebuilt Mathlib, builds the library and prints the axioms of its theorems. Each depends on
`propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` at most. Lean version: 4.35.0-rc3 (`lean-toolchain`); Mathlib: commit
c55e6e786f49 (`lake-manifest.json`). The library has 97 modules under `QECD1/`. It contains no `sorry`, no `axiom`
declaration and no `native_decide`.

## Main theorems

- `QECD1.d1B_num` (`QECD1/TheoremB3.lean`): for every `p ≤ 0.02`, `T ≥ 0`, `W ≥ 1` and `H ≥ 175`, the code with `T`
  noisy rounds and a perfect final round, `W` columns and `H` rows has maximum-likelihood failure probability at most
  `804 n_t n_x e^{-0.1008 (n_y - 47)} + 5.11e6 n_t n_x e^{-0.007 (n_y - 47)}`, where
  `n_t = max(5, ⌈(T + 1)/35⌉)`, `n_x = max(5, ⌈W/35⌉)` and `n_y = ⌊H/35⌋`. The hypotheses are (V6) (`S3Prog3.V6`),
  (Hcov) (`S3Fam3.Hcov3`), and the bound 1.85e-5 on the 25 bad-block probabilities at `p = 0.02`
  (`S3Fam3.Hstat3`). `QECD1.d1B_lean` gives the same bound with the unrounded constants.
- `QECD1.pML3_tendsto_zero_subexp`, `QECD1.pML3_exp_decay_subexp` (`QECD1/Threshold3.lean`): the threshold. When `W`
  and `T` grow subexponentially in `H`, the failure probability tends to 0 and is eventually at most `e^{-H/28000}`.
- `QECD1.d1B_certified`, `QECD1.threshold_d1_general_certified` (`QECD1/CertD1.lean`, `QECD1/Threshold3.lean`): the
  confidence statement. The binomial model of the 25 counts, condition (Bin), is the hypothesis `hlaw`; no bound on
  the bad-block probabilities is assumed.
- `QECD1.d1_accept`, `QECD1.d1_cp`, `QECD1.d1_cp_upper_k4`, `QECD1.d1_cp_tight_k4` (`QECD1/CertD1.lean`): the
  accepted counts satisfy the acceptance rule, and the quoted Clopper–Pearson upper bound is valid and correctly
  rounded up.
- `QECD1.fail_bound_prog3` (`QECD1/Fail3.lean`) is the failure bound of the auxiliary decoder, and
  `QECD1.gen_skel3_final` (`QECD1/LongA3Code3.lean`) its deterministic lemma.

`scripts/Axioms.lean`, whose output is `axioms_report.txt`, lists these theorems and the declarations they rest on,
849 in all.

## Names in the sources

- `d1B_lean`, `d1B_num` and `D1BConcl` name the bound on the maximum-likelihood failure probability, and
  `threshold_d1_*` the threshold.
- S3 and S3' denote the auxiliary (windowed) decoder. Its stage 1 is the registered program.
- `S3Prog3.V6` and `S3Fam3.Hcov3` are conditions (V6) and (Hcov); the paper's validations V2, V4 and V6 test them.
- "The 2D" in a docstring refers to the planar-code project of the same record (namespace `QECML`), whose modules are
  the counterparts of the modules in this project. `MLGen` and `CPGen` are copied from its generic parts.
- The tables in `QECD1/CertTab.lean` and `QECD1/CertData.lean` were generated by scripts that are not part of this
  tree; the kernel checks them (`CertTabCheck`, `CertCheck`).
